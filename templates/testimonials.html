About Us

Smart Blood Donor Finder is a web-based project designed to improve the way patients, hospitals and voluntary blood donors can connect during blood emergencies.

The platform allows donors to register their blood group, contact details, location and availability. Patients and hospitals can create blood requests and search for suitable donors.

👁️ Vision

To create a reliable and accessible digital platform that helps people find suitable blood donors quickly during medical emergencies.

🎯 Mission

To simplify blood-donor discovery, encourage voluntary donation and reduce delays in connecting patients with suitable donors.

🩸 Who Can Donate Blood?

Blood donation eligibility depends on age, weight, health status, medical history and applicable blood-bank rules.

A qualified blood-bank professional must perform the final screening before donation.

⚠️ Who Should Not Donate?

A person may need to postpone or may be excluded from donation because of illness, infection, certain medicines, recent medical procedures, pregnancy-related circumstances, low hemoglobin or other screening findings.

Final eligibility must always be decided by qualified medical staff.

Disclaimer & Privacy Policy

This project is intended to assist with donor and blood-request coordination. It does not replace hospitals, blood banks or qualified medical professionals.

Users should provide accurate information. Personal data and location information should be collected, stored and shared only with appropriate consent and security controls.
