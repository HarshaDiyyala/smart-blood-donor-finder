About Smart Blood Donor Finder

Smart Blood Donor Finder is an AI-assisted web platform designed to help people find suitable blood donors during normal and emergency situations.

The platform allows donors to register their blood group, location, availability and contact details. Patients or hospitals can create blood requests containing the required blood group, units, emergency level and hospital location.

The system can then search registered donors based on relevant matching information such as blood group, location and availability.

The main objective is to reduce the time required to identify potential voluntary blood donors and make blood-request coordination more organized.
